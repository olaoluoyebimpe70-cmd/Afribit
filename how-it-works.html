How AfriBit Gift Cards Work

From local currency to Bitcoin in four simple steps.

1

Choose Currency & Amount

Select any major African currency and the value you want on the gift card. Presets make it fast, or enter a custom amount.

2

Pay with Local Methods

Complete payment using Flutterwave (cards, bank transfer), mobile money (M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money) or direct bank transfer.

3

Receive Instant Code

As soon as payment is confirmed you get a unique gift card code (e.g. AB-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX). Copy it or save it in “My Cards”.

4

Redeem for Bitcoin

Enter the code on any supported crypto exchange, wallet or platform that accepts Bitcoin gift cards / vouchers and receive BTC in your account.
