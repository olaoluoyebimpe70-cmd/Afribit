Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AfriBit Bitcoin gift cards.

It is a digital voucher you buy with local African currency. After payment you receive a unique code that can be redeemed for Bitcoin on supported crypto platforms and apps.
We focus on currencies supported by Flutterwave and Paystack: Nigerian Naira (NGN), Ghanaian Cedi (GHS), Kenyan Shilling (KES), South African Rand (ZAR), Ugandan Shilling (UGX), Tanzanian Shilling (TZS), Rwandan Franc (RWF), West African CFA (XOF), and USD.
In a live version you would pay via Flutterwave (cards, bank transfers), mobile money providers (M-Pesa, MTN, Airtel, etc.) or direct bank transfer depending on your country.
Instantly after successful payment confirmation. The code appears on screen and is also saved under “My Cards”.
On any crypto exchange, wallet or website that accepts AfriBit (or partner) voucher codes. In a real deployment this would include popular African and international platforms that have integrated the redemption API.
